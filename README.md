## 🤝 The Concept
Everyday exchanges between friends, neighbors, and local creators often feel cold and awkward. **BizPay** reimagines community value exchange as an optimistic, transparent, and celebratory social interaction—where supporting a local maker or settling up after a group meal happens in seconds.

---

## ⚙️ The Technology
- **Angular & Ionic Capacitor:** Delivers 60 FPS tactile gesture physics across web and mobile devices.
- **Firebase Cloud Firestore Real-Time Synthesis:** Synchronizes peer-to-peer ledger state and celebratory confirmation animations across devices in under 100ms.
- **Dynamic QR Payload Encoding:** Generates scannable visual tokens for instant, camera-to-screen social exchanges.

---

## 🌱 The Impact
- Brings warmth, humor, and instant visual clarity to everyday community micro-interactions.
